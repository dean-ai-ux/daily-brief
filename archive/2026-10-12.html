AP via Detroit News · 3 min read

Oil tops $104 and the 10-year sits near a 24-year high

Brent jumped 4.2% to more than $104 a barrel on attacks on Gulf and Strait of Hormuz shipping, and the 10-year Treasury yield hit 5.29%, close to its highest since 2002. Stocks slipped as earnings season began.

Why it matters This is the backdrop for Wednesday's CPI (consensus 3.6% headline) and for whether the Fed hikes again on Oct 27–28.

Bloomberg Law

Trump says Warsh should have voted against September's rate hike

Trump told Time he 'probably would have voted against the board' in Warsh's place, but later said Warsh should vote the way he wants and blamed a 'very hostile board'. The Fed raised rates 25bp to 3.75–4.00% unanimously and penciled in another hike this year.

Why it matters The first open test of the new chair's independence, with a possible second hike two weeks away.

The Economist

Robert Hall and Michael Woodford win the economics Nobel

Two 'New Keynesians' are honoured for showing how policymakers can keep economies steady through shocks. Woodford's work is the intellectual basis of modern central-bank forward guidance.

Why it matters Timely irony: Warsh has vowed a 'quieter' Fed that gives markets fewer signals, which is a step away from Woodford's playbook.

Andrej Karpathy on X

Karpathy: the AI capability gap is now 'a sharp funnel'

His napkin math: about 6B people have barely touched LLMs, 1–2B use free chatbots, about 20M use frontier models professionally, and about 5,000 inside labs run agent swarms for weeks on large software projects. Human review is starting to fall behind.

Why it matters A useful mental model for judging AI claims, and a signal from inside a frontier lab (he joined Anthropic in May).

Y Combinator · Video discussion

YC partners: startups are moving from bits to atoms

Hard tech rose from 8% to 20% of the batch (robotics 1%→7%, manufacturing 4%→10%), solo founders from 5% to about 19%, and median end-of-batch revenue from about $8K to $20K MRR.

Why it matters The clearest read on where early-stage capital and talent are heading.
