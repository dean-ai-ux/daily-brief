# Dean's interests

Edit this file freely — the morning routine reads it every day. Plain English is fine.

## Who I am
I read this on my phone on the way to work, around 7:30–8:30am ET. I want a sharp
5-minute skim plus a short list of things worth my commute time. Smart, not breathless.
Tell me *why it matters*, not just what happened.

## The mix
I want a **blend**, not a Fed newsletter. Each day: the lead, then Markets & macro, AI & the new
thing, Startups & YC, Business stories, and one Wildcard. Fed/monetary policy gets **one item a
day** unless something big happens (decision, minutes, a major Warsh/Powell/Goolsbee speech).
Mix formats: always a couple of podcasts and a couple of videos.

## Topics (no single one should dominate)
1. **Macro** — inflation, jobs, growth, rates, bond and currency markets, energy shocks, trade.
2. **The Fed** — one item a day, as part of Markets & macro. Flag anything material from
   **Kevin Warsh**, **Jerome Powell**, or **Austan Goolsbee**; skip routine Fed chatter.
3. **Y Combinator** — important updates only: batch news, policy or program changes,
   notable YC companies raising or exiting, essays/talks from YC partners. Not every launch.
4. **Interesting industry stories** — how businesses actually work: strategy, competition,
   unit economics, founders' decisions, the surprising second-order effects.
5. **AI and "the new cool thing"** — what builders are excited about. Anything from
   **Andrej Karpathy** (talks, posts, videos, repos) is always worth flagging.

## Listening
- **Always consider:** Acquired, How I Built This, Odd Lots.
- **Odd Lots:** recommend when the guest is genuinely interesting — Goolsbee is the
  benchmark. A Fed official, top economist, operator, or an unusual expert = yes.
- **Also welcome:** a one-off episode from another show if a guest I follow appears
  (Warsh, Powell, Goolsbee, Karpathy) or it's clearly exceptional.
- Prefer episodes I can finish on a commute or across two (≤ 90 min) unless it's an Acquired epic.

## Watching
- **Karpathy** on YouTube — always.
- **Random YouTubers showing the new cool thing**: 1–2 picks from smaller creators (not
  mega-channels) demoing a new tool, technique, or build that's taking off this week.

## Reading
- **Sources I like:** WSJ, The Economist, NYT. Also fine: FT, Bloomberg, Stratechery,
  Matt Levine, good Substacks, primary sources (Fed speeches, papers).
- I subscribe to the big papers, so paywalled links are fine.

## Wildcard
One pick a day from outside these lanes — science, maths, history, design — if it's genuinely
excellent (a top Hacker News thread, a standout longread).

## Skip
- Celebrity, sports, horse-race politics, crime, culture-war fights.
- Hype with no substance; listicles; "5 stocks to buy".
- Anything I've already seen in the last week.
